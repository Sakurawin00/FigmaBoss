Frequently asked questions

How long does setup take?

Most teams are live in under 15 minutes. Add one snippet to your app or site, and Metrico starts tracking events automatically.

Do I need a data team to use Metrico?

No. The query builder uses plain English — filters, segments, and formulas are visual. Your PMs, designers, and support team can self-serve.

How is my data secured?

Metrico is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR-ready. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and Enterprise plans support data residency and bring-your-own-cloud.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel in two clicks from settings. Annual plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee — no questions asked.

What integrations are available?

40+ native integrations including Segment, Snowflake, BigQuery, dbt, Stripe, Slack, and Zapier, plus a full REST API and webhooks.
